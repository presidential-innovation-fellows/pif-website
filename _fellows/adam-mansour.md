**Joining PIF...**

In my work with Bay Area Housing Finance Authority and Google.org, I saw firsthand the impact that thoughtful product development can have on improving access to critical public services. I was especially drawn to the complexity and scale of these challenges, and to the opportunity to build solutions that directly serve vulnerable communities. The Presidential Innovation Fellows program felt like a natural extension of that work, and an opportunity to apply my experience in a broader public sector context.

**To work on...**

Helping the VA become an industry leader in AI that improves Veterans' lives, by delivering faster, higher quality, and more cost-efficient services, with strong governance and trust.
