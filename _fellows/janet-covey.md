**Joining PIF...**

A lot of my inspiration comes from my roots in museum and public-facing experience design — work that was always about helping people navigate complex information in ways that felt intuitive and human. That never left me. Now I want to bring that same thinking to challenges that affect people across the entire country, because good design should work for everyone.
