**Joining PIF...**

For me, it’s about taking my experience building software, architecting systems, and leading engineering teams at a fast-paced startup-to-IPO tech company, and applying it for public good. I’m motivated by the massive potential for impact in government and the opportunity to serve underserved communities.

I am a design lover at heart. While I’ve always been in deeply technical roles, I enjoy flexing into product management and user experience design, and it makes me a better engineer. I believe that good public technology is ultimately about meeting people where they’re at to help make their lives a little (or a lot) better. 

**To work on...**

Helping the VA adopt AI safely and responsibly in service of better patient outcomes. Bringing a human-centric approach: listening to clinicians and veterans to understand use cases rather than retrofitting technology to problems, building guardrails that let teams move quickly and safely, and helping define what good AI use looks like in healthcare and government.
