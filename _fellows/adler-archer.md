**Joining PIF...**

Having come from a military family, my instinct toward public service is what led me to enlist in the United States Air Force over a quarter of a century ago. Though I was obliged to leave the military after four years on active duty due to the Don't Ask, Don't Tell policy, my desire for public service never left. After 26 years building across defense, academia, health innovation, and entrepreneurship, PIF offered something rare and I couldn't pass it up. The chance to bring that full range of experience back into federal service at a moment when AI, digital health, and institutional transformation are converging. I wanted to be in the room where those decisions are being made.

**To work on…**

Delivering AI ready data sets, an AI platform for building tools, and applications for federal employees to support the effort to build energy infrastructure faster to secure American Energy Dominance and to power American industry in the global AI race.
